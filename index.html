Considered craft

Fifty years of seating, made in New Zealand.

We started building chairs in Hamilton in 1976. The factory is still on the same site, the warranties are still honoured by the people who shipped the chairs, and the joinery is still cut on machines we can walk over to.

The same chairs specified in 1996 are still being repaired and reupholstered for clients today. That's not a story — it's a service record.
